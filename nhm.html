National History Museum - Advertisement

This was my big project as part of my A2 project at the end of the year, in which I had to create advertisement posters for the Natural History Museum.

  • PurposeA2
  • RoleCreator
  • Year2026
  • ToolsPhotoshop, Illustrator

This was the main project in my A2 Project in which I had to plan and create an advertising campaign for the National History Museum.

When starting this project, I first had to look at the history of NHM's previous logos and advertising campaigns. After this, I then started to look at different pattern artists such as "Lefty Out There" & "Minoru Onoda"

After looking at different pattern artists, I then started to develop my own work in which I experimented with patterns in animal designs. This then led me to create a zebra pattern as a foreground pattern with an animal above a gradient in the background under the pattern.

I really liked the final outcome as it was easy to incorporate into different designs for Flags, Bags & Billboards.
