Volkswagen: Poster

This was a first-term college project in which I created a poster for volkwagen

  • PurposeCollege
  • RoleCreator
  • Year2025
  • ToolsPhotoshop, Illustrator

I started this project as part of my A1 College Assignment, where I had the option to create a poster for either the NHS or Volkswagen.

When creating this poster, I started by looking at Volkswagen's early advertising posters for the Beetle, which gave me the idea to create a Beetle with a shadow of a Beetle car.

I then experimented with different logo placements and whether to include a description of the car or a slogan such as "Unbeetable," a play on words for "unbeatable."

Overall, I was very happy with the final piece as it had lots of blank space, which gave it lots of meaning. Stages of development are shown below with different orientations as well.
