Volkswagen: Poster

This was a first term college project, In which I created a poster for volkwagen

  • PurposeCollege
  • RoleCreator
  • Year2025
  • ToolsPhotoshop, Illustrator

I started this project along with my A1 College Assignement, In which I had the option to either create a poster for the NHS or Volkswagen.

When creating this poster I started by looking at Volkswagen's first advertisement posters when they promoted thier beetle car, which gave me the idea to create a beetle with a shadow of a beetle car.

I then started experimenting with different placement of the logo and whether or not to include a description about the car or with a slogan such as "Unbeetable" which was a play on words to being unbeatable.

Overall I was very happy with the final piece as it had lots of blank space which gave it lots of meaning. Stages of development are shown below with different orientation as well.
