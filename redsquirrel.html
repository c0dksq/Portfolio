Red Squirrel: Poster / Christmas Card

All the work that I've created for Red Squirrel Property Shop.

  • ClientRed Squirrel Property Shop
  • RoleDesigner
  • Year2026
  • ToolsPhotoshop, Illustrator

This project started as a design for a local competition called the Ad-Mazing competition. It won 1st place overall, and I won my college £750. However, recently I designed Christmas cards for them with no brief whatsoever, then sent them physical cards and I am now working with them to design something for their upcoming campaign.

To design the original poster, the brief I had was to use their key beliefs of Honest, Trust, and Compassion. I used their logo and the key beliefs and mixed them as an overall with a noisy red background.

For the christmas card design I took the original design but changed the noisy red background for a snowy Christmas theme, in which I included a Christmas hat on top of the red squirrel. I also changed the bottom message to match a Christmas card.
